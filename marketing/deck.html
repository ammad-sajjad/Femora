Screen PCOS risk

A PCOS risk check in two minutes — and what she can change.

A short questionnaire about her body, cycle and lifestyle goes to a trained model that returns a risk band and the factors behind it.

  • Trained and testedXGBoost on self-reported features: 84.4% accuracy, 0.88 ROC-AUC on held-out data.
  • Shows the "why"BMI, hair growth, weight gain — the drivers appear as tags under the gauge.
  • What-if simulatorLive sliders show how her risk could respond to realistic lifestyle changes, within safe limits.

Not a diagnosisA risk-awareness estimate; every result points her to a gynaecologist or endocrinologist.

Screen Breast ultrasound

An ultrasound, checked by AI — with the area it looked at outlined.

She uploads a breast ultrasound image. A ResNet50 model classifies it, and a second model outlines the area the result is about.

  • Tuned to catch cancersAt its screening threshold it caught 89.2% of cancers on 710 held-out scans (AUC 0.87).
  • Three viewsSwitch between the original scan, the AI focus heatmap and the lesion outline (U-Net, Dice 0.86).
  • Refuses the wrong pictureA gate checks it really is a breast ultrasound before any result is shown.

Screening aidIt supports, never replaces, a radiologist's reading.

Track Cycle

Her cycle, predicted from her own history.

Log a period in one tap. Femora shows the phase, the next period, ovulation and the fertile window — and says when a cycle looks late or irregular.

  • Learns from herHer own average blended with a study average; after three cycles the next period was within 3 days about 82% of the time.
  • A calendar she can readPeriod, expected, fertile and ovulation days in one month view, with a note when a cycle runs long or irregular. Tap any day to log when a period started or ended.
  • Honest estimatesOvulation and fertile days are clearly marked as estimates, not contraception.

Understand AI companion

A companion she can talk to — out loud, in Urdu.

She taps how she feels or asks anything. The companion answers in Urdu or English, by voice or text, and knows her own results.

  • Voice in, voice outSpeech-to-text and a natural voice; the phone's own voice takes over if needed.
  • Knows her contextHer results guide the answers — her name is never sent to the model.

Connect Doctors

Privacy & safety

Designed to help her doctor — never to replace one.

Screening

Risk, not diagnosis

Every result is a risk estimate with a clear "see a doctor" next step.

Red flags

Emergencies caught in code

Danger signs trigger an urgent message and 1122 — the AI is not trusted with it.

Medicines

No doses, no prescriptions

Honesty
